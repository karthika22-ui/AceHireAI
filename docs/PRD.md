# AceHire AI - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Overview
AceHire AI ("Smart Interview Simulator AI") is an AI-powered placement preparation ecosystem designed for engineering and college students. 

### Key Innovation
**Dual-Language AI Feedback System**: Delivers feedback in standard **English** and accessible **Tanglish** (Tamil in English letters).

## Modules
1. **Authentication & Profile**: Target company selection (Zoho, TCS, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, etc.) & language preference.
2. **Dashboard**: Placement Readiness Score (0-100%), streaks, daily tasks, module progress.
3. **AI Mock Interview Simulator**: HR, Technical, and Company rounds with voice/text input and dual-language AI feedback.
4. **Resume Builder & ATS Analyzer**: Visual editor, upload support, ATS score gauge, and missing skill suggestions.
5. **Coding Sandbox**: Multi-language editor (Java, Python, C, C++, SQL) with test cases and AI code review.
6. **Aptitude Practice**: Quant, Logical, Verbal reasoning with instant explanations in English and Tanglish.
7. **Communication Coach**: Real-time grammar fixer and sentence enhancer.
8. **Skill Gap Analysis & Roadmap**: Compare skills against hiring requirements and generate daily action plans.
